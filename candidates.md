## Why the selected candidate wins

It turns a real, known failure mode — confident tone, wrong content — into an office drama: confidence and accuracy are two colleagues on different shifts who have never met, and the agent is the bad messenger between them. Specific mechanism, no stock phrasing, and the flat "badly" at the end does the comic timing.
